Privacy Policy

App: PaisaTrack - Personal Finance & Expenditure Monitor 100% On-Device Storage

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Data Collection and Financial Privacy

PaisaTrack is designed with privacy as the core priority. All your financial accounts, transactions, expenses, bank balances, credit card limits, loans, and investment portfolios are stored exclusively on your device using local device storage. None of your financial records are uploaded to, stored on, or processed by external company servers.

2. Internet Connection and Advertisements

Our application uses Google AdMob to display non-intrusive advertisements so that the application remains 100% free for all users without requiring a paid subscription. An active internet connection is required to fetch and display ads from Google and its certified advertising partners.

Google AdMob may collect and process standard advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), device information, and coarse location data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Security

Since your financial data never leaves your device, it remains as secure as your personal smartphone. We recommend protecting your phone with biometric lock (fingerprint/face) or a secure passcode.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach our team at: support@paisatrack.app
