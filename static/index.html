🔒 100% On-Device Financial Storage

PaisaTrack is architected for absolute privacy. All your financial accounts, bank cards, expense transactions, loans, and investment records are stored locally on your device. Your financial logs are never uploaded or sold to third-party data brokers.

📁 File Manager Database Persistence (Dedicated App Path)

To prevent accidental loss when uninstalling or changing phones, you can export and persist your entire database directly to this app's dedicated storage directory:

📍 Internal Storage/Android/data/com.paisatrack.app/files/database_backup.json

Files in this internal directory survive app uninstalls and can be inspected, edited, or restored across reinstalls.

⚡ Required Permissions & Why They Are Needed
🌐 Internet & Network State Required

Allows loading non-intrusive Google AdMob advertisements so that PaisaTrack remains 100% free with all premium features unlocked for life without subscriptions, and enables optional Cloud Backup sync.

💾 File Storage & Manager Access (Dedicated App Directory Only) Store & Restore Only

Exclusive Purpose: Access is granted strictly and solely for storing (export) and restoring (import) your local database backup .json file inside Internal Storage/Android/data/com.paisatrack.app/files/.

🛡️ Zero Access to Other Paths: We DO NOT scan, read, access, or touch ANY other directories, folders, photos, videos, contacts, or documents on your device. All storage operations are 100% isolated to this dedicated app directory.

🔔 Alerts & Carryover Reminders Optional

Used for month-end budget rollover reminders and credit card bill cycle due date notifications.
